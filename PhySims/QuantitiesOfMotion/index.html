Analyse

Position–time gradient is velocity. Velocity–time gradient is acceleration.

Explain

Signed area under velocity gives displacement. Total absolute area gives distance.

Model and definitions

This is ideal one-dimensional motion with constant acceleration, without collisions or track boundaries. Position x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at²; velocity v = v₀ + at. The cart may reverse once. Distance is calculated by splitting the journey at that reversal, rather than using the magnitude of displacement.

Displacement = final position − initial position. Average velocity = displacement / elapsed time. Average speed = distance / elapsed time. Instantaneous speed = |velocity|. At a zero-duration snapshot the averages are undefined and shown as a dash.

The drawn track auto-scales to include zero, both endpoints and any turning point. Graph axes remain fixed throughout playback. Motion is calculated from simulation time, not screen pixels. All measurements are exact model values, rounded for display; there is no experimental noise.
