Model assumptions: The gamma model uses representative half-value layers of 12 mm for lead and 18 cm for concrete. Alpha and beta use simplified representative ranges. Actual attenuation depends on radiation energy, material density and composition.

Learning mode

Key relationship

Penetrating ability and ionising ability generally move in opposite directions: alpha ionises strongly but travels only a short distance; gamma penetrates deeply but ionises less densely.
