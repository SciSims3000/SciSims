Transverse Wave Simulation

Investigate how amplitude, wavelength, frequency and wave speed affect a transverse wave. Watch particles move up and down while the wave pattern travels sideways.

Physics Waves Transverse waves Frequency

Learning Intention

Students will understand that a transverse wave transfers energy sideways while particles vibrate at right angles to the direction of travel.

Success Criteria

Students can identify crests, troughs, amplitude and wavelength, and explain how changing wave properties affects the wave model.

Student challenge

  • Increase the amplitude. What changes about the wave, and what stays the same?
  • Increase the wavelength. How does the spacing between crests change?
  • Increase the frequency. What happens to the period?
  • Turn on particle motion and describe how the particles move compared with the direction of the wave.
  • Pause the wave and identify one crest, one trough, the amplitude and the wavelength.

Teacher notes

This sim is useful for distinguishing wave motion from particle motion. Students often think particles travel along with the wave. The dots and arrows are designed to show that the particles vibrate up and down while the wave pattern moves horizontally.

  • Good prompt: What evidence shows this is a transverse wave?
  • Good prompt: Which control changes the height of the wave?
  • Extension: Ask students to use T = 1 ÷ f to check the period readout.
