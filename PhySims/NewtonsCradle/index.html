Controls

Start simple: release one ball, then compare it with two or three balls. Watch whether the same number of balls moves out the other side.

What the model shows

Momentum pattern: when 1, 2 or 3 balls are released, the same number should move out the other side.
Energy transfer: a higher collision efficiency keeps the cradle moving for longer.
Energy loss: as sound, heat and air resistance increase, the swing angle gets smaller.
Model limit: this is a simplified teaching model. A real Newton's cradle transfers momentum and kinetic energy while also losing some energy to sound, heat, tiny deformations and support movement. The sim represents those losses using the collision efficiency and air/support loss sliders.

How to use

  1. Choose how many balls to release.
  2. Set the starting angle and collision efficiency.
  3. Press Release and watch which balls move after the collision.
  4. Compare the animation with the energy graph and readouts.
  5. Use the CSV button to collect evidence for a short investigation.
