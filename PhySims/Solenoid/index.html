Model, method and limitations

The sensor stays on the axis at the centre of an air-core solenoid in stationary conditions. The model treats closely spaced turns as a continuous current sheet. There is no iron core, heating, resistance calculation, magnetic saturation or remanence.

Centre field: Bcoil = μ₀NI / √(L² + 4R²), where μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T m/A and R = 0.020 m. For L much larger than R this tends to μ₀(N/L)I. The finite-length expression gives the simulated readings; using the long-coil approximation introduces a small geometry-dependent difference.

Laboratory readings add the selected constant axial background and a new uniform random error to every reading. The diagram shows coil field only, not the total field sensed. Its field lines, loop count and end-view glyphs indicate direction and are not a quantitative field map.

Regression fits B = mI + b to recorded means, giving each current equal weight. For this geometry, Nestimated = (m/1000)√(L² + 4R²)/μ₀ when m is in mT/A. Error bars show sample standard deviation, not uncertainty in the mean. A non-zero intercept estimates the background in this model; in a real experiment sensor zero offset can also contribute.

Current reversal cancels constant background: [B(+I) − B(−I)]/2 isolates the coil field at +I. [B(+I) + B(−I)]/2 estimates background. Use equal current magnitudes and unchanged sensor position.
