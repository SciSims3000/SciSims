How to use

  • Use aperture to change how much light the telescope collects.
  • Use eyepiece focal length to change the magnification.
  • Move focus adjustment away from zero to blur the image.
  • Compare a refractor with a reflector and follow the ray path.
Challenge: Make the faint galaxy clearer. Does increasing magnification always help?

What the model shows

Telescopes do not just “zoom in”. Their first job is to collect light. The objective lens or primary mirror forms a small image at the focus. The eyepiece then makes that image easier to see.

In advanced mode, exit pupil estimates the width of the light beam leaving the eyepiece. If it becomes too small, the image can appear dim even when magnification is high.

Teacher notes
