Build-a-Cell Sorter

Sort cell structures into the cell types where they belong, then use evidence to compare and classify different cells.

Easy Mode compares plant and animal cells only. It focuses on shared eukaryotic structures and the plant-only features that help distinguish plant cells.

Learning intention

Students will understand that cell structures can be compared to classify different cell types.

Success criteria

Students can sort cell structures into the correct cells and justify their choices using evidence.

Classification clues

    Watch the common trap

    Plant cells have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole. Animal cells do not.

    Teacher note: Easy Mode uses a simplified school-level comparison of plant and animal cells. It focuses on commonly taught visible features rather than every structure found in every cell.
