Lick Your Rats

Explore how maternal licking and grooming in rats can affect gene expression, brain development and stress responses through epigenetic changes.

Biology Genetics Epigenetics Gene expression

Learning Intention

Students will understand that environmental factors can influence gene expression without changing the DNA sequence itself.

Success Criteria

Students can explain how maternal care in rats is linked to epigenetic changes, stress responses and differences in adult behaviour.
