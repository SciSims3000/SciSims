Round controls

All teams play at once. Type each team's nominated word, then lock its single attempt. At timeout, any typed answers lock automatically.

Match scores

How to play
  1. Choose a topic, teams and countdown. Press Start round.
  2. Build a science word from the nine letters. Each tile can be used only once; not every tile is needed.
  3. Each team has one locked answer. Answers and results are revealed together when the round closes.
  4. Check definitions, then accept or reject each word's relevance to the selected topic.
  5. The longest accepted word earns one point per letter. Shorter words earn zero. Tied winners each receive their full letter score.

Solo practice earns the accepted word's length. A dictionary match confirms an English word, not its science relevance. You can approve unfamiliar terms after checking a source, including terms the dictionary misses.

Single words only, 2–9 letters. Plurals and Australian spellings are allowed if valid science terms. Spaces, hyphens, abbreviations, chemical formulae and proper names are excluded.

Letter sets use a small collection of starter terms to ensure a playable science word. Those terms do not limit the answers you may submit. The revealed example is not a claim about the longest possible answer.
