    How to play
    1. Tap each die or drag it into your equation.
    2. Add operators and brackets. Normal order of operations applies.
    3. Use every die exactly once, then check.
    4. Try another solution or start a new puzzle.

    Keyboard: numbers 1–9, + − * / ( ), Backspace to remove, Enter to check. Tap a gap to insert, or select a token and choose Insert before/after. Use the editing buttons to remove or move it. Arrow keys move the insertion point; Delete removes the next token. Digits cannot be joined to form larger numbers.

    First eligible solve: 100 points, +50 without hints, +25 within 30 seconds. Streak bonus: +10 per earlier consecutive solve, capped at +50. A skip or timeout resets the streak. Revealed answers earn no points. Each puzzle earns points once in this browser; daily best times are recorded.

    Works offline. Scores are saved in this browser when available. Daily puzzles use the date in Australia/Sydney. Inspired by the supplied dice-target puzzle; original interface and game engine.
