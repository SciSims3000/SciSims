What the model shows

Earthquake protection systems do not all work the same way. Some make the building stiffer, some absorb energy, and some isolate the building from ground motion. A good design depends on the building, the earthquake and the ground conditions.

Protection guide:
  • Base isolation separates the building from some ground motion.
  • Cross bracing stiffens the frame and reduces sideways drift.
  • Viscous dampers absorb shaking energy and reduce repeated swaying.
  • Shear walls add strong vertical walls to resist sideways forces.
  • Tuned mass dampers use a heavy moving mass to oppose building sway.

This is a simplified conceptual model. Real earthquake engineering uses detailed structural models, material properties, building codes, soil data and safety factors.

Teacher notes

  • Use this after the seismograph simulator to connect earthquake evidence with engineering responses.
  • Ask students to keep one variable constant while changing the protection device.
  • The comparison mode supports CER writing: claim, evidence and reasoning.
  • The collapse animation is triggered when the simplified drift and acceleration risk becomes very high.
  • Emphasise that this is a model for pattern recognition, not a real engineering design tool.
