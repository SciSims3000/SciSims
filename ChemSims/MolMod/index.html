How to use the model

  1. Choose a guided challenge or switch to Free build. Free build is limited to one simple neutral covalent molecule at a time.
  2. Add the required atoms from the element palette.
  3. Select single, double or triple bonding, or choose Remove bond.
  4. Click one atom and then another to add, replace or remove the bond between them.
  5. Use Check molecule to test valency and compare your structure with the challenge.
  6. Drag across the Space-filling preview to inspect the molecule from different directions.

Model limitation: this model is designed for simple neutral covalent molecules. It does not represent ions, formal charges, radicals or resonance structures. Recognised examples use simplified VSEPR-style arrangements and a tighter perspective space-filling 3D preview in which bonded atoms touch or overlap slightly; unrecognised structures use an approximate display layout. Sulfur structures use simplified school-level bonding models.
