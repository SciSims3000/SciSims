How to use this activity

  • Start at methane, then press Build next carbon to add one carbon at a time.
  • Use Show hydrogens when you need to check why the formula changes.
  • Look for the pattern: each new carbon adds CH2 to the chain.
  • Complete the worksheet table using the name, carbon count, molecular formula and condensed structural formula.
Example: Butane has 4 carbon atoms. Its molecular formula is C4H10. Its condensed structural formula is CH3CH2CH2CH3.

Teacher notes

  • Core idea: alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons made from carbon and hydrogen only.
  • Formula pattern: straight-chain alkanes follow CnH2n+2.
  • Condensed structure: end carbons are CH3; middle carbons are CH2.
  • Common error: students often write CnH2n instead of CnH2n+2. Ask them to toggle hydrogens and count the atoms.
  • Limit of the model: the diagrams represent straight-chain alkanes only. Branched isomers are not shown.
