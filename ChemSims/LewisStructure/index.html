A reliable Lewis structure method

  1. Count all available valence electrons. Add electrons for a negative charge and subtract them for a positive charge.
  2. Choose a central atom and connect the atoms with single bonds.
  3. Complete the outer atoms first, then place remaining electrons on the central atom.
  4. If the central atom lacks an octet, convert neighbouring lone pairs into multiple bonds.
  5. Check electron totals, octets and formal charges. Some atoms have valid incomplete or expanded octets.

What the model shows

Lewis structures track valence electrons, but they do not show molecular size, bond length, three-dimensional shape or electron-cloud distribution accurately. Compare completed structures with the Virtual Molecular Model Kit when teaching molecular geometry.
