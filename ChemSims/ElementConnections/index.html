Element Connections

Sort 16 chemical elements into four connected groups of four. Connections may involve periodic-table families, physical properties, chemical behaviour, technology, living systems, Earth resources, history or atomic-number patterns.

Learning Intention

Students will recognise patterns in the periodic table and connect elements through shared properties, chemical behaviour, uses, origins and environmental roles.

Success Criteria

Students can sort elements into four defensible groups, explain each connection and use symbols, atomic numbers and periodic-table position as evidence.

How to Play

  1. Select four elements that share one clear connection.
  2. Press Submit Group. Correct groups lock into place.
  3. Continue until all four groups are solved.
  4. Click a solved element to inspect its atomic details.

Teacher Notes

  • Classic Families introduces metals, non-metals and major periodic families.
  • Themed banks focus revision on one area; Mega Mix combines every bank.
  • “One away” feedback appears when three selected elements belong together.
  • Each retained puzzle has one complete four-group solution within its bank and complexity level.
  • If students select a scientifically valid quartet that cannot complete the curated board, the game acknowledges it without taking a mistake.
