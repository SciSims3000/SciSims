Learning Intention

Students will understand that medical imaging technologies are selected by matching the clinical question to the strengths, limitations and risks of each technology.

Success Criteria

Students can compare X-ray, CT, MRI and ultrasound, justify an imaging choice for a scenario and identify ethical issues such as radiation exposure, consent, access and unnecessary testing.

Ethical and practical considerations

Risk–benefit

Use the lowest-risk technology that can answer the clinical question. Ionising radiation may be justified in emergencies, but should not be used unnecessarily.

Access and equity

MRI and CT may not be available quickly in all locations. A lower-detail but available test may be the safest first step in some settings.

Consent and privacy

Patients should understand why the scan is needed, major risks, alternatives and how sensitive medical images and results are stored.
