Drag tables from their coloured centre surface and drag room objects directly. In Planning mode, use the rotate button or table editor to angle tables in 45° steps for open-plan arrangements.

Planning mode and seating rules

Planning notes and rules are teacher-only and hidden from the full-screen student display. Notes are stored locally but are not encrypted.

Custom-room tables can be assigned to coloured learning zones. Roles are distributed among students seated in each zone.
