How to play

1. Start on a coloured numbered endpoint.

2. Drag through adjacent squares, or tap squares one at a time.

3. Connect its matching endpoint. Paths cannot cross or share squares.

4. Fill the entire grid to win.

Science note: These lines are a logic-puzzle representation of relationships. They do not represent real electrical wiring, energy-transfer directions or food-web arrows.
