How to play

1. Start on a numbered endpoint, or pick up an existing path to edit it.

2. Drag through adjacent squares, or tap squares one at a time.

3. Connect its matching endpoint. Paths cannot cross or share squares.

4. Fill the entire grid to win.

Stars: ★★★ without hints, ★★ with 1–2 hints, ★ with more. Score rewards grid size and fewer moves, hints and seconds; a correct science answer adds 500. Best results are saved separately for each mode.
