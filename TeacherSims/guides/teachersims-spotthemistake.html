Discussion & reasoning · Teacher guide

Spot the Mistake

Identify an error, correct it and explain why the correction matters.

Suggested time: 5–15 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose a bank, topic, statement count, play mode and thinking time. For Teams, enter 2–6 names, one per line.
  2. Start round. Students write a correction or explain it aloud before Reveal & compare.
  3. Assess with Corrected + explained (2), Partly correct (1) or Not yet (0), then choose Next statement.

Try it in class

Use a mini-whiteboard correction from every student before discussing the model answer.

Save and reuse

Export results CSV at the end. Custom banks stay in this browser; Export selected bank provides a portable JSON copy.

Tips and troubleshooting

Written answers are not automatically marked. Accept scientifically equivalent wording, but require reasoning for full credit.
