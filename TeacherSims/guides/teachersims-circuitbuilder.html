Science games · Teacher guide

Circuit Builder

Investigate how circuit arrangement affects current, voltage and brightness.

Suggested time: 15–25 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose an early mission or Free build. Select a component and tap a square, or drag it into the workbench.
  2. Lay wires, rotate components as needed and Test design. Inspect components and live readings.
  3. Make one change at a time and compare the result with a prediction.

Try it in class

Compare two bulbs in series and parallel, using current and voltage readings to explain brightness.

Save and reuse

Builds and challenge progress stay on this device. Record circuit sketches and readings in student notebooks.

Tips and troubleshooting

Dotted components are anchored. A warning may indicate a short circuit. Bulbs use fixed resistance in this model; real filament resistance changes with temperature.
