Classroom routines · Teacher guide

Drift — Soft-Fascination Simulator

Offer a quiet classroom reset with a natural scene.

Suggested time: 2–5 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose an environment and Single or Drift mode.
  2. Leave sound off or enable it at a suitable volume. Enable countdown only if you want a timed session.
  3. Select Start. Press H to hide controls; Pause or Reset when needed.

Try it in class

Use a brief reset between demanding tasks, with no response or score required.

Save and reuse

Use this as a live display. Keep preferred scene and duration in your lesson notes.

Tips and troubleshooting

Countdown is optional. If audio is silent, click to enable sound and check device volume.
