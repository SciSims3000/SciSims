Particle Detective

Support claims about matter with particle-model evidence.

Suggested time: 15–25 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose a case and read the brief and model key.
  2. Observe the particle display and collect the requested evidence before making a claim.
  3. In investigation cases, change one variable at a time and compare results before answering.

Try it in class

Ask students to separate observations from inferences and explain which evidence supports their classification.

Save and reuse

Progress stays on this device. Record observations, controlled variables and claims in a science notebook.

Tips and troubleshooting

The particles are a model, not a literal microscopic view. Use the case’s evidence requirements rather than guessing from colour alone.
