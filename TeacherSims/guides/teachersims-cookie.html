Classroom routines · Teacher guide

Science Fortune Cookie

Use a light science prompt as a starter, transition or discussion hook.

Suggested time: 2–5 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose Fortune type, such as Science fact, Study tip or Mystery challenge.
  2. Select Crack the cookie, click the cookie or press Space.
  3. Ask students to explain, test or question the prompt; use Crack again for another.

Try it in class

Ask: Is this a factual claim, advice or an imaginative prediction? What would count as evidence?

Save and reuse

Recent fortunes appear in the on-screen history. Copy a useful prompt into your lesson notes if you need to retain it.

Tips and troubleshooting

Ridiculous predictions are humour, not scientific forecasts. Choose the fortune type to suit the lesson.
