Animation Lab

Use a short animation to prompt prediction and explain a science idea.

Suggested time: 5–10 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose a course or branch, focus area and animation. Use the catalogue to browse ready-made MP4s.
  2. Play, Restart or adjust Speed; select the available animation settings.
  3. Ask students to predict before playback, then revisit the prediction using the science explanation.

Try it in class

Pause a wave animation and ask what moves: the disturbance, the marked particle, or both?

Save and reuse

Ready-made MP4s are linked in the catalogue. Custom MP4 export requires the local Animation Lab helper on your Mac.

Tips and troubleshooting

A caption entered here is burned into exported video frames. Read the model limitations; diagrams and timing may be illustrative.
