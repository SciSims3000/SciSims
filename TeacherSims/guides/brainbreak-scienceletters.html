Science games · Teacher guide

Science Letters

Retrieve science vocabulary and justify its relevance.

Suggested time: 10–15 min

Open tool

Quick start

  1. Choose topic, solo or 2–6 teams, countdown and answer visibility. Select Start round.
  2. Each team builds one 2–9 letter science word and locks its attempt. Use each letter tile at most once.
  3. End round & reveal; review definitions and topic relevance, then Award points.

Try it in class

After scoring, ask teams to explain their word using an example from the current topic.

Save and reuse

Match scores save in this browser when available. Import JSON, Download template and Export custom bank support your own topic banks.

Tips and troubleshooting

Dictionary checking needs internet. Teacher review remains available. The longest accepted word earns points; a dictionary match alone does not establish science relevance.
