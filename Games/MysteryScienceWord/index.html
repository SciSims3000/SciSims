Mystery Science Word

Guess the hidden science word. Use the clue only when needed, and try to solve the word before the unstable science artefact falls apart.

Learning Intention

Students will understand that key science terms help scientists describe investigations, evidence, data and explanations clearly.

Success Criteria

Students can identify introductory science vocabulary from letter patterns, clues and definitions, then explain the meaning of each term.

Definition

Student Challenge

  1. Play one round without opening the clue.
  2. If needed, reveal the clue and continue guessing.
  3. After the word is solved, write the definition in your workbook.
  4. Use the word in one clear science sentence.
  5. Choose one solved word and explain why it is important in science.

Teacher Notes

  • Topic controls the word bank. Difficulty controls the number of lives.
  • All Words randomly selects from the built-in introductory science list.
  • Teacher Custom Words uses only the words imported through the bulk import box.
  • The clue is hidden by default and can be toggled during play.
  • The hint button reveals the first letter and the word-length pattern.
  • Class score mode can be used for Team A versus Team B revision rounds.

How to Import Custom Words

Paste one word per line into the bulk custom word box. Each line must use this structure:

word | clue | definition

Example:

fair test | An investigation where only one variable is deliberately changed. | A fair test controls variables so the result is more valid. hypothesis | A testable prediction. | A hypothesis predicts the relationship between variables. peer review | A process where other scientists check research before publication. | Peer review helps improve the quality and reliability of scientific research.

After importing, choose Teacher Custom Words in the topic dropdown, then select New Word. Custom words stay available while the page remains open.
