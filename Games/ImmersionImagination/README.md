# Immersion & Imagination — Aster Reach

A fictional observatory mystery for practising observation, inference and revision of explanations. Students keep a physical expedition diary, reconstruct the chart and explain their choices. The teacher map is an optional story spoiler, not an astronomical chart.

Progress and reading preferences are saved on this browser. Restart begins again after confirmation. Reading options provide larger text and light/dark themes. Three endings are available after the dome is opened.

Published as a prototype in Interesting Sims and supporting Stage 4 unit 7.1.
