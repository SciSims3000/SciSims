Learning Intention

Students will understand that energy vocabulary helps explain energy transfers, transformations, circuits, efficiency and future energy systems.

Success Criteria

Students can identify 9.1 Energy vocabulary from letter patterns, clues and definitions, then explain each term in a scientific context.

Student Challenge

  1. Play one round without opening the clue.
  2. If needed, reveal the clue and continue guessing.
  3. After the word is solved, write the definition in your workbook.
  4. Use the word in one clear sentence about energy.
  5. Choose one solved word and explain why it matters in the 9.1 Energy topic.

Teacher Notes

  • Topic controls the word bank. Difficulty controls the number of lives.
  • All Words randomly selects from the full 9.1 Energy vocabulary list.
  • Teacher Custom Words uses only the words imported through the bulk import box.
  • The clue is hidden by default and can be toggled during play.
  • The hint button reveals the first letter and the word-length pattern.

How to Import Custom Words

Paste one word per line into the bulk custom word box. Each line must use this structure:

word | clue | definition

Example:

entropy | A measure of how spread out energy is in a system. | Entropy describes how energy becomes more spread out over time. power station | A place where electricity is generated. | A power station converts another form of energy into electrical energy. fossil fuel | A non-renewable fuel formed from ancient living matter. | Fossil fuels include coal, oil and natural gas.

After importing, choose Teacher Custom Words in the topic dropdown, then select New Word. Custom words stay available while the page remains open.
