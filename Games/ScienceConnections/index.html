Science Connections — Introduction to Science

Find four groups of four related science terms. Each group shares a scientific connection.

Learning Intention

Students will organise scientific vocabulary into meaningful conceptual groups.

Success Criteria

Students can identify relationships between terms and explain the scientific connection within each group.

Definition

Student Challenge

  1. Select four terms that share one scientific connection.
  2. Submit the group. Correct groups lock into place.
  3. Find every group before the mistake counter reaches zero.
  4. After solving a group, select any term in it to review its full definition.
  5. Explain why each term belongs in its group using a complete scientific sentence.

Teacher Notes

  • Standard puzzles contain four vocabulary categories; Insane puzzles contain six. Every category has four terms.
  • Specific modules draw categories from that module. Year Review draws from all modules in the selected year.
  • Mega Mix draws categories from across Years 7–10 and uses the bright-pink theme.
  • A “one away” message appears when three selected terms belong to the same category.
  • Hints identify two connected terms. Easy mode also names their category.
  • Category terms are cycled before being reused where possible.
