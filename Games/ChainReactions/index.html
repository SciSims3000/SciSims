Explanation

Student Challenge

  1. In Science Sequence mode, drag the terms or use the arrow buttons to place them in order.
  2. Explain why each step follows the previous step.
  3. In Chain Reaction mode, start with the given term and build the longest valid chain.
  4. Use Undo Last Link when a valid branch leads to a shorter route.
  5. Review the definitions and relationship explanations after completing each challenge.

Teacher Notes

  • Easy sequence rounds use four terms; medium rounds use up to six; hard rounds use the complete curated sequence.
  • Easy chain rounds show a smaller candidate pool. Medium shows the full network. Hard adds plausible distractors from the selected module.
  • A chain may contain branching pathways. The target is the longest valid pathway available from the starting term.
  • Year Review selects from all curated challenges in that academic year. Mega Mix selects from all Years 7–10 and uses the bright-pink theme.
  • The vocabulary definitions come from the shared word banks used by the other SciSims word games.
