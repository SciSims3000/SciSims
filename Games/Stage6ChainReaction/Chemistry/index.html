Student Challenge

  1. In Science Sequence mode, drag terms or use the arrow buttons to place them in order.
  2. Explain why each step follows the previous step before checking.
  3. In Chain Reaction mode, begin with the supplied term and build the longest valid chain.
  4. Use Undo Last Link when a valid branch leads to a shorter route.
  5. Review the accepted relationships and definitions after each challenge.

Teacher Notes

  • Easy sequence rounds use four terms; Medium uses six; Hard uses the complete curated sequence.
  • Easy chain rounds use a direct five-term pathway. Medium includes the full branching network. Hard adds plausible distractors.
  • Chain links are deliberately curated: conceptual similarity alone is not automatically accepted.
  • Whole-course review combines Modules 5–8. Mixed Expert integrates synthesis, equilibrium, structure and analysis.

Content aligned to the NSW Chemistry Stage 6 Syllabus (2017). Definitions and relationship explanations are concise classroom paraphrases.
