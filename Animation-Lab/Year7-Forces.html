Each video has three stages and lasts 24 seconds at 1×. The ready-made silent MP4s are 1280 × 720, H.264, 30 fps. Use the shared player to change theme, palette, speed or add a short caption, then export again.

The set covers direct and indirect forces, balanced and unbalanced forces, gravity and orbits, weight calculations, electrostatics, magnets and magnetic fields, electromagnets, forces and energy, simple machines and design. It includes an example of Aboriginal technological knowledge, linked to a museum source. The plain linkage illustrates a mechanical principle, rather than reproducing a particular cultural object.

Force arrows belong to the object they act on. Some horizontal diagrams omit balanced vertical forces; captions state the model limits. Machines are idealised unless noted. These explainers support practical investigations, data interpretation and classroom discussion rather than replacing them.

Sources: NESA Science 7–10 (2023), OpenStax: forces, OpenStax: simple machines, Pitt Rivers Museum: spear-throwers. Individual background links are also in the player.
