How this prototype works

The library loads each animation’s settings automatically. Choose a theme and palette, then customise the wave colours if needed. Branch tags allow one animation to appear in several parts of the library. A small drawing module implements each concept; the player and exporter are shared. Palette choices apply to diagram colours and video captions. Big Bang’s cosmic glow retains its illustrative colours. MP4 exports are silent 1280 × 720 H.264 videos at 30 frames per second, starting at the beginning.
