Each science explanation has three stages, captions and a 24-second MP4. Theme, palette, speed and optional captions can be adjusted and exported in the player. Diagrams exaggerate sizes, distances, shadows and tidal bulges for clarity.

This set supports nature and practice of science, solar-system models, Sun–Earth–Moon patterns and sky observations. It supports classroom teaching rather than replacing practical investigations or certifying every content point. The cultural knowledge animation uses an invented star pattern and a source-linked Torres Strait research example; it does not recreate a community’s story or calendar.

Sources: NSW teaching programme, NESA syllabus, NASA Moon science, Torres Strait star-twinkling research.
