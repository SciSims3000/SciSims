SciSims Animation Lab — science animation library

OPEN
Open index.html in Safari or Chrome, or use the local helper at http://127.0.0.1:8765/. The player opens in light mode with Year 7, 7.4 Solutions and Mixtures selected, showing the rebuilt solids/liquids/gases model. Dark mode, five palettes, and individual wave colours are available.

COURSES AND TOPICS
221 unique animations, including 60 Year 7 explainers:
7.1 Observing the Universe — 12
7.2 Forces — 16
7.3 Cells and Classification — 14
7.4 Solutions and Mixtures — 18
Year 7 topics support the NESA Science 7–10 Syllabus (2023), Stage 4. The numbering is this library’s teaching sequence, not compulsory NESA Year 7 module numbers.

The existing HSC library remains available under InvSci, Bio, Chem, Phy and Earth, with course-specific modules 5–8. Its mappings follow the 2017 Year 12 syllabuses. There are 93 HSC subject explainers plus earlier Investigating Science, Working Scientifically, Big Bang and wave entries. Cross-branch topics are shared without duplicating animations.

Use Course / branch, Module / focus area and Find to narrow the collection. These core-concept explainers support teaching rather than certifying every syllabus dot point, practical or assessment requirement. Diagrams and data are illustrative; captions state important limits. Sources are linked in the player and topic guides.

CONTROLS
Play/pause, restart, timeline scrub and speed are shared. Most explainers have three stages over 24 seconds at 1×, with Science explanation and Learning sequence / Context views. Historical investigations also have independently drawn science explanations. The four original Working Scientifically entries and original Big Bang/wave retain their durations. Optional captions appear in the preview and exported video.

VIDEOS FOR SLIDES
Catalogue.html is the searchable course/module video index. Year7-Videos contains 60 captioned MP4s. HSC-Videos contains 93, and InvSci-Videos contains 56 earlier videos. Other original examples are in the main folder. New videos are silent H.264 MP4, 1280 × 720, 30 fps, 24 seconds, light theme and classic palette.
Upload MP4s to Google Drive, then in Google Slides use Insert > Video > Google Drive. Viewers need access to the video file.

EXPORT ANOTHER VERSION
Double-click Start Animation Lab.command and leave its Terminal window open. Choose animation, view, theme, palette, speed and optional caption; click Export MP4. A download link appears when finished. Generated files are also saved in exports. Exports start at the beginning and omit player controls. Stop the helper with Control-C.
If the command file does not launch, open Terminal, type python3 and a space, drag server.py into the window and press Return.

The local helper uses this Mac’s Chrome, Python 3, FFmpeg and Codex-bundled Node/Playwright. It uploads nothing. Other computers need dependency configuration. The HTML player and ready-made videos work independently of the helper. Manual JSON loading/saving is not needed.
