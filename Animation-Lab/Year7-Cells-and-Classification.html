Each science explanation has three stages over 24 seconds at 1×. Ready-made MP4s are silent H.264, 1280 × 720 at 30 fps, with explanatory text embedded in the video. Use the player to change theme, palette, speed or add a short caption and export another version.

Figures are schematic and not to scale. Synthetic data and model limits are identified in the captions. The collection supports teaching and practical investigations rather than certifying every syllabus content point or replacing laboratory work. Cultural examples use generic diagrams and require an attributed, publicly shared account when studying a particular community.
